Add emoji role badges: `#emojiBadge` in the `social.colibri.beta.role` badge union, `roleEmojiBadge` and `roleEmojiBadgeView` in `social.colibri.beta.community.defs`, and the optional `emojiBadge` input on `social.colibri.beta.role.create` and `social.colibri.beta.role.update`. A badge holds either a Unicode `emoji` or a `customEmoji` reference.
